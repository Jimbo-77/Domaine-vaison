Les Cigales, a house for 4 people with two terraces in Vaison-la-Romaine

The largest house of the Résidence Le Théâtre Antique: 65 m² and two private terraces facing south and east, on a gated estate with a pool, a large garden and a pétanque court.

  • 65 m² for 4 people
  • From €750 per week, cleaning and linen included
  • Town centre a 300 m walk

Photos

The house

Les Cigales sleeps 4 people in 65 m²: a bedroom with a double bed, a second bedroom with two single beds, a living room with television and Wi-Fi, a bathroom with a shower and an equipped kitchen: washing machine, microwave, fridge, coffee machine.

Its two private terraces, facing south and east, are made for enjoying the Provence sunshine. Sun loungers are provided, and your hosts will lend you the barbecue. A baby cot and an extra bed can be added.

A welcome basket awaits you on arrival. End-of-stay cleaning, bed linen and towels are included in the price.

The estate is closed by a gate. The 10 × 5 m pool, the large garden, the pétanque court and the car park are shared by the three houses of the Résidence. The Roman Theatre is a stone's throw away, and the centre of Vaison-la-Romaine a 300 m walk.
